**IAS 29 Mechanics:**
Turkish inflation (TÜFE) exceeded 100% on a 3-year cumulative basis in 2024, triggering mandatory application of IAS 29 (high-inflation accounting). Under IAS 29:
- Non-monetary assets and liabilities are revalued for cumulative inflation
- Monetary assets/liabilities (cash, receivables, debt) are NOT revalued
- **Parasal zarar** arises when a company holds net monetary assets during inflation (cash loses purchasing power)

**FY2024 Spike Explanation:**

**FY2025 Normalization:**
- IAS 29 still active but on a forward basis (not retroactive restatement)
- Inflation moderating (though still elevated); accumulated purchasing power losses already taken in FY2024
- Parasal kazanç impact minimal (89M); real earnings closely align with reported

**Downstream Implication:**
- **For DCF modeling:** Use net income without the IAS 29 drag. Adjusted EBIT / (EBIT + D&A deduction) more reliable for terminal value.
- **For peer comparison:** Turkish peers (if any) face identical IAS 29 adjustment; global peers do not. Be careful in cross-border multiples.
