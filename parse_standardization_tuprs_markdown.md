| **DPO** (Days Payable Outstanding) | 30–45 days | 30–50 days | ⬆️ Aligned peer | Sektör ortası |
| **CCC** (Cash Conversion Cycle) | **5–15 days** | **15–30 days** | ⬆️↑↑ Excellent | **Çok olumlu — Nakit yaratma makinesi** |
| **NWC / Sales** | ~8% | 10–15% | — | Efficient WC deployment |

### Working Capital Efficiency:
- **Short CCC:** 5–15 day cycle means TUPRS collects from customers (20–25 days) before paying suppliers (30–45 days)
- **Negative Operating Cycle:** Effectively operates on supplier credit; receives payment from customers → pays suppliers with lag
- **Competitive Advantage:** Pompa fiyatı (pump price) indexing allows rapid demand-based pricing; customer payment immediate
- **Implication:** As sales grow, working capital actually *releases* cash (inverse of normal); supports dividend payments without asset buildup
