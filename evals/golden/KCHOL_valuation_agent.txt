**Formula:** P = D₁ / (Ke - g)

**Where:**
- D₁ = Expected next dividend
- Ke = Cost of equity
- g = Perpetual growth rate

**Inputs:**

- FY2025 dividend: 6.83 TRY
- Growth assumption: 5% nominal (conservative; below historical but sustainable)
- **D₁ = 6.83 × 1.05 = 7.17 TRY**

**Ke (Cost of Equity):**
- Risk-free rate (Turkey 10-yr): 30%
- Equity risk premium: 8%
- **Ke = 38%**

**g (Perpetual Growth):**
- Turkey long-term nominal GDP growth: 10-12% (6-7% real + 5% inflation)
- KCHOL sustainable growth = ROE × retention ratio = 3.25% × 21.3% = **0.69%**
- **Conservative g = 3%** (between sustainable 0.69% and GDP growth 10-12%)

**Fair Value:**
