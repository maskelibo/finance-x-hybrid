Koç Holding owns Turkey's best industrial and financial assets: a monopoly refiner (Tüpraş), the leading automotive exporter (Ford Otosan), the #7 bank (Yapı Kredi), and the dominant white goods producer (Arçelik). **If you could buy these assets directly at their market prices, you'd pay 854.51B TRY. **Why the discount?** Because the holding company structure **destroys value**:
- ROE 3.25% (vs. 38% cost of equity) = **-34.75% economic loss** per year
- ROCE 1.48% = capital is **mis-allocated** across unrelated businesses (banking + refining + automotive makes no operational synergy sense)
- Family control (63.4%) with dual-class shares = **minority shareholders have limited influence** on capital allocation improvements

**The opportunity** is that management **knows this**. Recent actions prove it:

**The question** is whether these moves **signal a trend** (sustained portfolio optimization → ROE improvement → discount compression) or are **one-off transactions** (selling into strength, no structural change).
